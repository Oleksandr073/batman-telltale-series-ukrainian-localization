# Names glossary — Batman: The Telltale Series (S1)

**One English name = one translation, in every file.** Fill in the `Translation` column once and then always use it.
When a name is declined/inflected in the target language, write the base form here and keep the stem consistent.

- `Count` = how many times the exact English form appears in the visible text of the default-mode (BM_*) files
  (credits and writers' notes excluded). Noir files repeat the same text.
- Speaker IDs in header lines (`43) VICKI`, `BATMANBRUCE`, `ENFORCERCOLLEEN`) are **not** translated. Never touch them.
- Real people in `ui_credits*` (developers, actors) stay in Latin letters, as published.
- Possessive `’s` and family plurals (`the Waynes`, `the Vales`, `the Arkhams`, `the Cobblepots`, `the Falcones`,
  `the Zellerbachs`, `the Dents`) follow the same translated stem.
- Source typos (bottom table) are translated as the **correct** name.
- Translations below are first suggestions (G → г, H → г: Готем, Гордон, Гарві). Change any of them here,
  before they are used in the table. Inflect as normal Ukrainian (Брюса, Брюсові, Брюсе; Готема).
- Cobblebot (joke on Cobblepot): Кобблбот.
